Admission Guidelines

Candidates must fulfill the following prerequisites to register for the upcoming session:

  1. Submit the online registration form.
  2. Upload high school certificate transcripts.
  3. Clear the institute's entrance review examination.
  4. Participate in the personal interview phase.
  5. Pay the seat booking fees to lock registration.
